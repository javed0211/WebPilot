Test: Search for Hotels on Booking.com

1. Navigate to https://www.booking.com/
2. If a cookie consent dialog is visible, accept or dismiss it
3. Verify the Booking.com logo and accommodation search form are visible
4. Enter "London" in the destination field
5. Select London, United Kingdom from the destination suggestions
6. Open the date picker
7. Select a check-in date at least 7 days from today
8. Select a check-out date 2 days after the selected check-in date
9. Click the Search button
10. Verify the search results page is displayed
11. Verify the destination is London and accommodation results are visible
